**Grow → Store presence → Main store listing**. Character counts are Google's
limits; the counts in brackets are what these drafts actually use.

## Content rating questionnaire

Answer it in Play Console; the rating is generated from the answers, and getting
it wrong is a common rejection. For a shopping app with no user-generated
content, no ads and no gambling, expect **Everyone / PEGI 3**.

Answer **yes** to: "Does your app allow users to purchase digital or physical
goods?"

If you later add product reviews written by customers, you must re-take the
questionnaire — that is user-generated content.
