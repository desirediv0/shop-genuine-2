## What we collect

**When you create an account:** your name, email address and phone number. We
send a one-time code to your email to confirm it is yours.

**When you place an order:** the delivery addresses you save, what you ordered,
what you paid, and the status of each order.

**As you use the app:** the contents of your cart and wishlist, and the store
you last browsed. Before you sign in, your cart is held only on your own device
and is sent to us when you sign in.

**If you allow notifications:** a push token identifying your device, so we can
tell you when an order is placed, dispatched or cancelled. The app works fully
without this and you can turn it off in your device settings at any time.

**Technical information** needed to serve a request, such as your IP address.

## What we do not collect

**We never see your card, UPI or netbanking details.** Payments are handled
inside Razorpay's own secure checkout. Those details go directly to Razorpay and
never pass through the app or our servers. We receive only a payment reference
confirming the payment succeeded.

We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your data.

## Why we use it

- To create and secure your account
- To take, deliver and invoice your orders
- To tell you what is happening with an order
- To answer you when you contact support
- To meet legal obligations, including retaining tax invoices

## How long we keep it

Your account details are kept while your account exists. **Order records and
invoices are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires, even after you
close your account** — see below.

## Your choices

**You can delete your account from inside the app**, at Account → Delete my
account. No email to support is needed. You can also request deletion at
[SUPPORT EMAIL].

Be aware of exactly what happens, because it differs depending on your history:

- **If you have never placed an order**, your account and personal details are
  deleted outright.
- **If you have placed an order**, your name, email and phone are removed from
  our records and your sign-in is permanently disabled, but the order itself is
  kept. We are required to retain invoices, and an invoice cannot exist without
  its order.

You can also change your password, edit or remove saved addresses, and turn
notifications off, all from inside the app.

## Children

Shop Genuine is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly
collect data from children.

## Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are
stored hashed, never in plain text. Payment gateway credentials are stored
encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to
protect your information.
